# Predictions waiting for observations {#predictions}

A prediction is useful only if it says which observation would prove it wrong. **Table 1** lists
the predictions of the test reports with that observation. Columns:

- *system*;
- *prediction*: what SWINGERsim says, with its uncertainty where one exists;
- *how to test it*: the observation that would confirm or refute it;
- *where*: the report and section with the evidence.

Dates are calendar years. Ranges are 16–84 % of an ensemble or 95 % of a posterior, as stated.
