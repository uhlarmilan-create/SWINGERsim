# Purpose of this document {#purpose}

The User Guide and the Physics and Methods volume describe what SWINGERsim is and how it works. The
four test reports describe what it reproduces:

- Part I: published benchmarks and catalogue systems;
- Part II: BU CMi, TIC 120362137 and V994 Her;
- Part III: V907 Sco, QX Cas and AH Cep;
- Part IV: CzeV343, CzeV4315 and the convergence of the fits.

This document collects what those reports leave open. Its sections are:

1. **Predictions waiting for observations** – statements about the future or about data not yet
   taken, with the data that will confirm or reject them ([Section 2](#predictions)).
2. **Open questions** – disagreements and weak points found in the tests, each with what would
   settle it ([Section 3](#open)).
3. **Possible extensions of the software** – what SWINGERsim does not do today and what an
   extension would need ([Section 4](#extensions)).
4. **Research and student projects** – questions SWINGERsim is ready for
   ([Section 5](#projects)).
5. **Questions for colleagues** – items that need data or expertise from outside the project
   ([Section 6](#colleagues)).

Each item names the document and chapter where the evidence is described. Nothing here is a claim
beyond those reports; this is a working list.
